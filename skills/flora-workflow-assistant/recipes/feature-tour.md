# Feature tour

**Makes:** a canvas that shows a team what FLORA can do, built with their own assets. Good for training, a workshop or a first look.
**Start with:** their logo, a few products or pictures, a short description of their work.
**Shape:** a menu, not a chain. One capability per group, 80 to 100 boxes. Do not use the block-per-shape layout here.

## Blocks (one group each)
IMAGE GENERATION, IMAGE TO IMAGE, TEXT TO IMAGE TO VIDEO, CASTING WITH ELEMENTS, TRY ON OR PLACEMENT, APPLYING A STYLE, BATCH, TECHNIQUES, ACTIONS, LAYERS, COLOR GRADING, VFX, IMAGE TO 3D, VOICE / MUSIC / LIP SYNC, TIMELINE.

## Empty slots
Their assets in every group. Each group's example comes from their world.

## Watch out
- Plain CAPS names. No numbering, no shape notation, no notes by default.
- Every feature stays in, even if it makes the canvas big.
- Short, dry asks. Never leave a PLACEHOLDER in something that will be presented.
- Some boxes (Batch, 3D, Elements, document boxes) may need to be made by hand. List them at the top of the plan, not at block 7.
- Do not run anything until they name a box and accept its price.
- Start from a finished canvas of the same kind if the person has one: copy whole groups and swap inputs, models and copy.

## Cost note
Build and wire for free. Quote each box they might run. Video boxes cost the most.
