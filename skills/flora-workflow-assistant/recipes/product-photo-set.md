# Product photo set

**Makes:** 8 to 12 views of one product (front, three-quarter, side, detail, in use) that look like one shoot. For a product page or a catalogue.
**Start with:** 3 to 7 real photos of the product (front, back, side, close-up of the logo or label), on a plain background if possible.
**Shape:** execute. Every reference wired into every generator (ladder: "Many views of ONE thing").

## Blocks
1. **THE PRODUCT:** the real photos as image boxes, each labelled by what it shows ("front", "label close-up").
2. **THE SHARED PROMPT:** one holder with the invariant prompt (product as in the references, plain sweep, soft key light, true colour, one photograph) and avoid bullets. See "shared part + one changing line" in `grammar.md`.
3. **THE VIEWS:** one image box per view. All references wired into each. Each box has its own SHOT line (angle, distance) and the shared holder wired in.
4. **SET CHECK:** one text box that reads every frame against a lettered rubric (`finish-and-checks.md`). Wire it last.

## Empty slots
The product photos. The SHOT lines they want. The background colour.

## Watch out
- Say what each reference is the truth of ("image 1 is the truth of the product: take nothing else from it, not its crop or pose").
- Logos and small text drift. Check them at 100%, not on a contact sheet.
- "no grid, no contact sheet, no turnaround" in the avoid bullets, or you may get one image with many views.
- Count copies: "one product, no second copy".

## Cost note
Twelve image runs at 2K on a top image model. Estimate before running; the list price can be well under the billed price.
