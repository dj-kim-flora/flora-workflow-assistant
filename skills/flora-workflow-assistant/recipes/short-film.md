# Short film

**Makes:** a 20 to 60 second film from 3 to 6 stills: clips, sound and a cut.
**Start with:** the stills (real renders or photos from one consistent set), and a voice line only if the person wrote one.
**Shape:** execute. One holder per shot (ladder: "A film").

## Blocks
1. **THE STILLS:** real images from one set so light, materials and people match.
2. **SHOT LIST:** one holder per shot, no model. Each says why the shot exists and what the camera does: *"shot 2 of 4, the approach. seven seconds. a slow push in from a tripod. nothing is added; only the people walk."*
3. **CLIPS:** one video box per shot, still and holder wired in. Two models only on the hero shot.
4. **AMBIENCE:** one sound-effect box per clip, wired into that clip.
5. **MUSIC BED:** one music box for the whole cut, on the timeline, not on a clip.
6. **VOICE (only if written):** text-to-speech at about 2 words a second, under 30 words for 15 s.
7. **TIMELINE:** clips in order, bed underneath. Export from the UI.
8. **DRIFT CHECK:** one check box that compares first and last frames.

## Empty slots
The stills. The shot list. The voice line, if any.

## Watch out
- Arrive, enter, hero, detail, leave is a shot list. Four camera moves is not.
- Two stills plus one motion sentence is a toy, not a film.
- Current video models only.
- If the canvas cannot carry the full film, build one honest shot with its sound and say what the full film adds.
- Timeline export is a UI click.

## Cost note
Clips dominate. Estimate each, total it, wait for a yes.
